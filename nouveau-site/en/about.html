Our DNA

Accelerator of telecom, energy and IT network modernization

IGTSD supports GSM operators, integrators, TowerCo, regulators, civil works firms and enterprise accounts in redesigning critical infrastructure. Our positioning is field-built: 4G/5G densification, FTTH/FTTX migration, energy resilience, urban rerouting and modernization of operating models.

From site acquisition to commissioning, we align project governance, telecom engineering, civil works, IT cabling and power solutions to deliver networks that are ready to operate. Every program is framed by SLA, HSE, regulatory compliance and executive reporting commitments.
