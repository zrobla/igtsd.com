Our DNA

Enabler of telecom and energy network modernization

IGTSD supports GSM operators, integrators, TowerCos, enterprise accounts and public institutions in redesigning critical infrastructure. Our positioning is field-built: 4G/5G densification, FTTH/FTTX expansion, energy resilience and transformation of operating models.

From site acquisition to commissioning, we align project governance, telecom engineering, civil works and power solutions to deliver networks ready to operate. Every program is framed by SLA, HSE and compliance commitments.
