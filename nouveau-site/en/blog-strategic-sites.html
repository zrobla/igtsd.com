Key points to decide and execute

This analysis outlines the operational levers to secure rights-of-way and accelerate commissioning without regulatory drift or field risks.

Contents

  1. Key points
  2. Business impacts

Key points

  • Land and legal due diligence on critical sites.
  • Proactive coordination with authorities, local councils and landowners.
  • Energy qualification and site access constraints assessment.
  • HSE compliance and regulatory prerequisites management.

Business impacts

  • Shorter site-securing timelines.
  • Fewer field blockers and rework events.
  • Better predictability of CAPEX/OPEX schedules.
  • Faster commissioning milestones.
