Overview

Who are we?

IGTSD is an Ivorian company that supports operators, TowerCo, regulators, construction and public works stakeholders, and enterprises in executing their network, energy, and civil engineering programs; we combine project management, KPI-driven governance, field execution control, technical acceptance, and documentation aligned with international standards; we deliver measurable business outcomes through faster service rollout, tighter CAPEX/OPEX control, and sustained improvement in service availability.

Mission
