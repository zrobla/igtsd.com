Overview

Who are we?

IGTSD is an Ivorian company that supports operators, TowerCo, regulators, construction and public works stakeholders, and enterprises in executing their network, energy, and civil engineering programs; we combine project management, KPI-driven governance, field execution control, technical acceptance, and documentation aligned with international standards; we deliver measurable business outcomes through faster service rollout, tighter CAPEX/OPEX control, and sustained improvement in service availability.

Mission

Measurable sector execution

Turn technical objectives into business outcomes through controlled commissioning, documented compliance and lower project risk.

Competitive edge

Why work with IGTSD?

An organization built around delivery, regulatory compliance and business impact for strategic telecom and critical infrastructure programs.

Field expertise

  • Experienced teams across mobile networks, fiber, energy, urban rerouting and critical infrastructure.
  • Strong control of multi-stakeholder constraints involving operators, TowerCo, local authorities, regulators and civil works contractors.
  • Operational culture focused on HSE safety, contractual quality and sustainable performance.

Integrated execution

  • Unified value chain from land studies to commercial commissioning.
  • Continuous coordination between engineering, civil works, fiber, energy and operations.
  • Fewer handoff gaps between framing, delivery, handover and run.

Project governance

  • Decision-oriented steering committees with rapid arbitration and CAPEX/OPEX prioritization.
  • Quality governed through QA/QC milestones, KPIs, runbooks and traceable action plans.
  • Consolidated reporting for technical, operations, finance and governance leadership.

Compliance & HSE

  • HSE standards enforced across the full field intervention lifecycle and urban worksites.
  • Compliance control over deliverables, acceptance files and regulator requirements (ARTCI/ARTP).
  • Contractual alignment with client expectations, regulators and local stakeholders.

Delegated project ownership

  • Cross-functional steering of stakeholders, milestones and delivery risks.
  • A single governance framework aligning technical, operations, procurement and finance teams.

Benefits of delegated project ownership

Schedule

Tighter milestone control, faster arbitration and fewer planning slippages.

Cost

Continuous CAPEX/OPEX tracking, tighter deviation control and fewer rework costs.

Performance

Stronger SLA/SLG commitments through quality gates, escalations and traceable KPIs.

Business impact

  • Faster time-to-market for priority FTTH, RAN and energy programs.
  • Lower post-deployment rework costs and lower non-compliance exposure.
  • Continuous improvement in availability, perceived quality and resilience.
  • For operators: improved QoE, lower churn and stronger monetization of data usage.
  • For TowerCo, integrators and enterprises: stronger contractual performance and higher operational value of critical infrastructure.
