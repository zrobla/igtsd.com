Delivery method

Results-driven methodology

IGTSD organizes rerouting delivery from impact study to compliant handover so that works can advance without unnecessary outages.

01

Impact framing and interface mapping

Decision phase

We qualify the affected assets, service criticality and stakeholder interfaces before approving the intervention strategy.

Deliverables: impact note, interface map
02

Switching and compliance design

Design phase

We define sequencing, switch windows, fallback logic and documentary obligations to secure the intervention envelope.

Deliverables: switch plan, compliance matrix
03

Field coordination and rerouting execution

Delivery phase

We coordinate utilities, civil works and network teams during execution to protect safety and service continuity.

Deliverables: execution log, coordination minutes, updated plans
04

Final conformity and service stabilization

Performance phase

We validate as-built status, restore documentary conformity and confirm service stability after the intervention.

Deliverables: as-built pack, conformity records, stabilization report
